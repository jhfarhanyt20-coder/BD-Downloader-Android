**VibeDownloader for Android 1.0.0** - the first release.

Download **VibeDownloader-1.0.0.apk** on your Android phone (Android 8 or newer) and open it to install. From now on the app updates itself: new versions download in the background and you tap **Install now**.

**What it does**
- **Share to download:** in YouTube, Chrome or any app tap Share → VibeDownloader. A small sheet opens on top; pick a quality and keep watching while it downloads.
- **Video and audio:** choose Best, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p or Audio only.
- **Fast file downloads** split into parts, with pause, resume and automatic resume after the network drops or the phone restarts.
- **Many links at once:** paste or share several links and add them all to the queue.
- **Built-in browser** with a Download button on video pages.
- **Smart download button** that appears only when it is useful.
- **Downloads by type** (Video, Audio, Documents, APK), duplicate check, storage overview.
- **Dark, Light or System theme**, choose your download folder, and notifications with Pause, Resume, Cancel, Open and Share.
